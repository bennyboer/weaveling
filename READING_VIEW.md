# Reading view ideas

The idea is two-fold: a reading view for logged in members of a project. Today the reader role can't really do anything because they cannot see the writing view -> now they get a reading view (also all other members of the project). The goal is a pleasant reading experience. I think there should be two modes: one that looks similar to the writing view and one that looks similar to a real book with pages, white background, ...

Then the other side of the reading view is the external reading view for non-project members, aka guest, aka external or anonymous users. The project owner AND authors should be able to share a link to the whole book, one or multiple consecutive sections or one or multiple consecutive scenes. The link can be shared with multiple people. Those people should be able to read the shared text, leave feedback on individual passages (selections of text), whole scenes or whole sections (or the whole book).

## Open questions

- Can you review/leave feedback as logged in user/member to the project in the internal reading view as well?
- Where can the authors see the feedback given via the external reading view?
- Once a text is shared, is that a frozen version or does it update live when the author changes something? Or is that configurable?
- Can the reading experience be configured? Fonts, colors, ...?
- Do we save the reading progress for readers in the external reading view? Perhaps related: once they leave the link, can they revisit the page and see their own feedback?
- Share by link or also share by e-mail address?
  - How does sharing look for logged in users? Does it look any different?
