# feedback

This feature keeps what readers say about something in the work. A piece of feedback is about a **subject**, for now a reading copy, and a **place** in it, typed for each kind of subject (`AboutDTO`): in a reading copy a passage, a scene, a section or the whole copy (`ReadingCopyPlaceDTO`). Who may see it follows from the subject. Feedback starts as a draft only its writer sees, and is submitted later. See M18, slice 2, in the [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md).

- `GET /feedback/drafts?…` lists the asker's own drafts, oldest first, narrowed as `/feedback/on` is (`project=`, `subjects=`, `places=`); a draft has no status, so `status=` is refused as `feedback-status-unknown`. The reading view's column asks it beside `/feedback/on?subjects=reading_copy:…` for what everyone submitted.
- `POST /feedback` drafts a piece of feedback about what `AboutDTO` says. A passage lies within one block: the scene, the path to the block in it (`block_path`: its place among the scene's blocks, then inside a quote or a list item its place there), a stretch of its text as offsets in characters, and the quoted text, so it can be shown without the copy. What it says is a **comment** of our own blocks, drawn as a reading copy's text is: paragraphs and lists, their runs in italics, in bold or linked, a line broken by a run of its own (`CommentBlockDTO`, `CommentRunDTO`). Never HTML, so nothing a reader writes runs in another's browser. A passage holding no character is refused as `feedback-passage-empty`; a comment saying nothing, longer than 10,000 characters over all its runs, with a control character in a run, or with a link anywhere but a page on the web or an address to mail, as `feedback-comment-blank`, `-too-long`, `-control-character` or `-link-unsafe`.
- A draft is begun and submitted only while its copy is open, as the readings told it; on a copy closed by now it is refused as `feedback-reading-copy-closed` (409).
- `POST /feedback/submitted?…` submits the asker's drafts together, narrowed as `/feedback/drafts` is (`subjects=reading_copy:…` from the reading view), so everyone who may see their subject's feedback sees them, and answers with what was submitted; with no drafts, nothing.
- `GET /feedback/counts?subjects=reading_copy:a,reading_copy:b,…` counts the feedback on many copies at once, at most a hundred (`COUNTED_AT_ONCE`; more are refused as `feedback-too-many-counted`): for each, in the order asked, the feedback submitted and not done yet, and the asker's own drafts. The reading view's switcher asks it for the copies the readings listed.
- `GET /feedback/on?…` lists the feedback submitted, drafts left out, oldest first, asked as `AskedDTO` in the contract tells: `?project=p&subjects=reading_copy:c1,reading_copy:c2&places=scene:a,scene:b,section:s`. Every parameter named narrows what the others leave (in `p`, and on one of the subjects, and at one of the places, and given by one of the givers, `given_by=account:<id>,…`); the values inside one parameter are alternatives; a parameter left out does not narrow. A subject is what feedback is given on as a whole, `reading_copy:<id>`; a place is where inside it, in a copy's text `scene:<id>`, on the scene or passages in it, or `section:<id>`, on the section itself, so a section with everything in it is the section and its scenes. Nothing named is refused as `feedback-place-unnamed`, a kind not known as `feedback-subject-unknown` or `feedback-place-unknown`; at most a hundred copies, scenes and sections at once (`COUNTED_AT_ONCE`, more refused as `feedback-too-many-counted`). With `status=pending` or `status=done` only that is found; anything else is refused as `feedback-status-unknown`. It is paged by `order=oldest|newest` (anything else refused as `feedback-order-unknown`), `after=<feedback>` going on after that piece, and `limit=n`, at most a hundred. The scene's and the section's inspector ask it for their own; the writing view asks it for what is pending on the scenes it draws alone, so a long book with much feedback is not loaded whole; the feedback view pages through a project's, newest first. *The stub knows a copy's project by hearing `reading-copy.made`; step 5 keeps it.*
- `GET /feedback/pending?…` counts the feedback given and not done yet, narrowed as `/feedback/on` is (`project=`, `subjects=`, `places=`, `given_by=`; neither a status nor a page), by scene (on it or a passage in it) and by section itself, leaving out what has none (`PendingDTO`), and how much in all (`total`); the writing view's contents and the scenes' flags show it, and the *Feedback* tab its total.
- `GET /feedback/givers?project=…` lists whoever gave feedback in a project, its givers, in the order they first submitted any (`ListedGiverDTO`): how `given_by=` names them (`account:<id>`, or `local` on an instance without accounts), whether it is the asker, and their name where the feedback knows it. *The stub knows no names yet; step 5 keeps them, givers from outside the project too (`guest:<id>` beside `account:<id>`).* The feedback view's *By* picker lists them.
- `PUT /feedback/{feedback}/done` marks a piece submitted as done, `DELETE /feedback/{feedback}/done` as not done again, answering with the piece; a draft is not there for it. *Who may mark it, the project's authors and owners, comes with step 5.*
- `PUT /feedback/{feedback}/comment` rewrites what it says, marking it *edited* once it was submitted, and `DELETE /feedback/{feedback}` deletes it, for whoever wrote it. Someone else's draft is not there for anyone else; someone else's submitted feedback is refused as `feedback-not-yours`.

*For now the routes run on a stub that keeps drafts in the server's memory, so the reading view can be built on them first; who may read the copy, whether it is open, and the `Feedback` aggregate come in step 5.*

**Crates:** `core` (`FeedbackCore`, `About`, `Subject`, `ReadingCopyPlace`, `BlockPath`, `Comment`) · `contract` · `adapters/rest` · `adapters/catalog` (`InMemoryReadingCopyCatalog`: the copies heard of, their project and until when they are open) · `adapters/messaging` (`feedback-remember-reading-copies`, kept in the inbox, hearing `reading-copy.made`, `.extended` and `.deleted`) · `wiring` · `tests`.
